**Full voiceover:**
"Cancer nanomedicine is usually scattered: a paragraph in drug delivery, a slide in imaging, a future-directions section in oncology. That is the problem. Tumor biology determines delivery. Particle chemistry determines circulation and clearance. Imaging determines whether delivery can be measured. Pharmacology determines whether the payload helps more than it harms. Regulation determines whether a platform becomes medicine. The gap book is not a catalog of particles. It is the framework across scale, transport, measurement, and treatment."
