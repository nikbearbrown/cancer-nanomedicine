**Full voiceover:**
"A nanoparticle is not a tiny pill. It is a tiny delivery system. Nano starts with scale, but the point is architecture. A particle can carry cargo, protect it in blood, change where it distributes, target tissue, release payload, or carry an imaging signal. That matters because tumors are uneven, leaky, pressured, and hard to reach. Cancer nanomedicine asks: how do we get the right payload to the right place, at the right time, with acceptable toxicity?"
