**Full voiceover:**
"Getting a drug to a tumor is not like mailing a package. First, a nanoparticle has to survive in blood without being cleared. Then it has to leave abnormal tumor vessels. Then it has to move through dense matrix and high interstitial pressure. Then it has to reach the right cell and enter if entry is needed. Then it has to release the payload at the useful time. Every step loses dose. Nanomedicine is hard because the target is a hostile transport environment."

**Asset prompts:**
- "Nanoparticle delivery barriers in solid tumor: blood clearance, leaky vessel, dense extracellular matrix, high interstitial pressure, cell uptake, payload release."

**Caption copy:** The target is not just a cancer cell. It is a transport environment.
