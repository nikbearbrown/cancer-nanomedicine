**Full voiceover:**
"A liposome is basically a tiny lipid bubble. It can carry drug inside or in the membrane. In cancer therapy, that shell can change how long a drug circulates, where it distributes, and what toxicities dominate. That does not make the drug magic. It changes the delivery problem. The lesson from liposomes is that nanomedicine often succeeds first by improving the therapeutic window, not by perfectly targeting every cancer cell."
